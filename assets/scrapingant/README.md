# ScrapingAnt sponsor logo

`scrapingant-logo.png` is ScrapingAnt's own brand mark, downloaded from the
`Organization.logo` field of the JSON-LD structured data on
<https://scrapingant.com> (`https://scrapingant.com/images/scrapingant.png`),
i.e. the canonical logo ScrapingAnt itself publishes for the sponsor callout in
`README.md`, `README.ko.md`, and `README.es-ES.md`. It is used here solely to
identify ScrapingAnt as a jeo-skills sponsor per the terms in
[`docs/scrapingant-partnership.md`](../../docs/scrapingant-partnership.md).
